# Welcome to the VlinderCLI Blog

We're excited to launch the VlinderCLI blog! This is where we'll share project updates, deep dives into architecture decisions, tutorials, and tips for getting the most out of VlinderCLI.

<!-- more -->

## What to expect

- **Release notes** — highlights from each new version
- **Architecture deep dives** — how time-travel debugging, the queue system, and distributed deployment work under the hood
- **Tutorials** — step-by-step guides for common workflows
- **Community** — contributions, showcases, and ideas from users

Stay tuned for more posts. In the meantime, check out the [documentation](https://docs.vlindercli.dev/) to get started.
